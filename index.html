What is Games On Demand?

This is the place to play indie tabletop RPGs! Games on Demand focuses on small press and independent games. Our games often feature unique mechanics and settings from a wide range of creators. Talk with the Hosts to learn more about what's available and choose what's most exciting to you.

New 2-hour sessions start every hour, with offerings changing continuously throughout the weekend. Want a longer experience? 4-hour sessions can be reserved in advance with a specific GM — check the schedule below for availability.

Our GMs are prepped and ready to go! We love teaching first timers but experienced players will always find something new. Head to the Tabletop Events Hall on Summit Level 0 to join a game.
