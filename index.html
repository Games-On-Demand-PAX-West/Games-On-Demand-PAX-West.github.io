What is Games On Demand?

This is the place to play indie tabletop RPGs! Games on Demand focuses on small press and independent games. Our games often feature unique mechanics and settings from a wide range of creators. Talk with the Hosts to learn more about what's available and choose what's most exciting to you.

New 2-hour sessions start every two hours, with offerings changing throughout the weekend. Pick any game on a GM's list for that slot. Want a longer experience? Our 4-hour games are scheduled on demand — talk to the desk and we'll find a time that works for you and the GM.

Our GMs are prepped and ready to go! We love teaching first timers but experienced players will always find something new. Head to the Tabletop Events Hall on Summit Level EX to join a game.

Available on demand

These longer games aren't on the fixed schedule. Ask at the desk and we'll set one up at a time that works for you, subject to GM availability.
